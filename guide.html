Your Questions, Answered

🏛️ Heritage Guide

Quick answers to the questions visitors ask most about George Town. Tap a question to open it.

What is George Town and why is it special?
George Town is the historic capital of Penang, Malaysia. In 2008 UNESCO named it a World Heritage Site for its unique multicultural heritage — 500 years of Chinese, Indian, Malay and European communities living and trading side by side. Read the full history →
What is UNESCO and why does it protect George Town?
UNESCO is the United Nations' Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, founded in 1945 to build peace through culture and education. It protects places of "Outstanding Universal Value" for all humanity — and George Town is one of them. Learn why we have UNESCO →
What is the oldest temple in George Town?
The Goddess of Mercy Temple (Kuan Yin Teng), built in 1728, is the oldest Chinese temple. The oldest Hindu temple is Sri Mahamariamman (1833), and the oldest mosque is the Kapitan Keling Mosque (1801).
What are the "must-see" heritage sites?
Top picks: the Blue Mansion, Khoo Kongsi clan temple, the Chew Jetty water village, the Kapitan Keling Mosque, and the street-art murals. See all 17 sites →
What are the Clan Jetties?
They are floating villages of wooden houses built on stilts over the sea by Chinese immigrant clans in the late 1800s. Families still live there today. Visit Chew Jetty →
What is the "Street of Harmony"?
It is the nickname for a short stretch of road where a mosque, a Chinese temple, a Hindu temple and a church all stand within a few minutes' walk — a symbol of George Town's peaceful multicultural mix.
How do I get around and find directions?
The old town is small and best explored on foot or by trishaw. Every site on this website has a "Directions" button that opens Google Maps. Open the Directions page → or follow our suggested walking route →
Is George Town good for a school or family trip?
Yes! It is compact, safe and packed with history, colourful temples, street art and famous food. Our walking route links nine of the best sites into an easy half-day tour.
What food is George Town famous for?
Penang is considered one of Asia's great food cities — try char kway teow, assam laksa, cendol and Nyonya (Peranakan) dishes. Little India and the Clan Jetties are great places to eat and wander.
