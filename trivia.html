Heritage Trivia

A bank of 50 questions about UNESCO and George Town, split into 5 rounds of 10. Pick a round, then tap an answer to see instantly if you're right.

Choose a round below. Selecting an answer reveals the correct one and an explanation. Reload the page to reset.

Khoo Kongsi's present hall was rebuilt in 1906 after a...

✓ The grand hall burned in 1901 and was rebuilt by 1906.

Dr. Sun Yat Sen is regarded as a founding father of modern...

✓ He helped found modern China.

George Town was named after which British king?

✓ It was named after King George III.

The Chew clan originally worked mainly as...

✓ They loaded and unloaded ships at the port.

In which year was George Town inscribed as a World Heritage Site?

✓ George Town was inscribed in 2008.

The Penang State Museum building was originally a...

✓ It began in 1821 as part of the Penang Free School.

George Town was inscribed together with which other city?

✓ The listing is 'Melaka and George Town'.

What is George Town's romantic nickname?

✓ George Town is called the Pearl of the Orient.

Han Jiang Ancestral Temple belongs to which Chinese community?

✓ It is the Teochew clan temple.

The Clan Jetties are villages built on stilts over the...

✓ They are built over the sea at the harbour's edge.

The 'Street of Harmony' is famous because on it you find...

✓ Four faiths' buildings stand within a short walk.

Little India is the historic quarter of which community?

✓ It is the Indian commercial and cultural quarter.

Which is the largest and most famous of the Clan Jetties?

✓ Chew Jetty is the largest.

How many selection criteria are there for World Heritage sites?

✓ There are ten criteria; a site must meet at least one.

What does UNESCO stand for?

✓ UNESCO = United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization.

What shape is Fort Cornwallis?

✓ It is a star-shaped fort.

Which Lithuanian artist sparked George Town's street-art fame in 2012?

✓ Ernest Zacharevic painted the 2012 'Mirrors George Town' murals.

George Town is the capital of which Malaysian state?

✓ George Town is the capital of Penang.

Cheong Fatt Tze was nicknamed the...

✓ He was called the 'Rockefeller of the East'.

The Acheen Street Malay Mosque has an unusual ___ minaret.

✓ Its Egyptian-style minaret is octagonal.

St. George's Church is the oldest ___ church in Southeast Asia.

✓ Built in 1818, it is the oldest Anglican church in Southeast Asia.

What gives the Blue Mansion its blue colour?

✓ Its walls use a rare indigo-blue lime dye.

Zacharevic's most famous George Town mural shows children on a...

✓ 'Kids on a Bicycle' is his most famous mural.

A site can be listed only if it has...

✓ Sites must show 'Outstanding Universal Value'.

Nagore Dargha Sheriff is a shrine honouring a...

✓ It honours the Sufi saint of Nagore.

Roughly how large is George Town's protected Core Zone?

✓ The Core Zone is about 109.4 hectares.

The Pinang Peranakan Mansion was the home of which wealthy leader?

✓ It was Kapitan Chung Keng Quee's mansion.

In which year was UNESCO founded?

✓ UNESCO was founded in 1945, just after World War II.

Kapitan Keling Mosque was founded by which community?

✓ It was founded in 1801 by Indian Muslims.

Penang joined Melaka and which city to form the Straits Settlements?

✓ The Straits Settlements were Penang, Melaka and Singapore.

What is the oldest Hindu temple in George Town?

✓ Sri Mahamariamman was built in 1833.

Francis Light worked for which company?

✓ Light worked for the British East India Company.

The Acheen Street area was nicknamed the 'Second ___' for pilgrims to Mecca.

✓ So many pilgrims sailed from here it was called the 'Second Jeddah'.

What is the oldest Chinese temple in George Town?

✓ The Goddess of Mercy Temple was built in 1728.

The Goddess of Mercy Temple is dedicated to...

✓ It honours Kuan Yin, the Goddess of Mercy.

UNESCO's famous register of protected places is called the...

✓ Places are inscribed on the World Heritage List.

In which city is UNESCO's headquarters?

✓ UNESCO is headquartered in Paris, France.

Who founded George Town in 1786?

✓ Francis Light landed in 1786.

The 'Straits-born Chinese' are also known as...

✓ They are the Baba-Nyonya, or Peranakans.

Fort Cornwallis marks the spot where who first landed?

✓ Light landed here in 1786; the fort is named after Charles Cornwallis.

Which festival's procession begins at Sri Mahamariamman Temple?

✓ The Thaipusam chariot procession starts there.

What is a 'kongsi'?

✓ A kongsi is a clan association.

In which year was the World Heritage Convention adopted?

✓ The World Heritage Convention was adopted in 1972.

A shophouse on Armenian Street was the Penang base of which revolutionary?

✓ Dr. Sun Yat Sen based his 1911 revolution work there.

UNESCO is a specialised agency of which organization?

✓ UNESCO is a UN specialised agency.

Why was UNESCO created?

✓ UNESCO builds peace in people's minds through education and culture.

The Kapitan Keling Mosque is built in which architectural style?

✓ It has Mughal-style golden domes.

Cheah Kongsi sits on which famous street?

✓ Cheah Kongsi is on Armenian Street.

After a site is listed, the country agrees to...

✓ Countries commit to protect listed sites for the future.

The indigo 'Blue Mansion' is properly called the ___ Mansion.

✓ It is the Cheong Fatt Tze Mansion.

Popular questions
Which is the oldest?
The oldest Chinese temple is the Goddess of Mercy Temple (1728). The oldest Hindu temple is Sri Mahamariamman (1833), and the oldest mosque is the Kapitan Keling Mosque (1801).
