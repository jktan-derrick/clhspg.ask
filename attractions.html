Popular questions
Which is the oldest?
The oldest Chinese temple is the Goddess of Mercy Temple (built 1728). The oldest Hindu temple is Sri Mahamariamman (1833), and the oldest mosque is the Kapitan Keling Mosque (1801).
Directions
Every site has a "Get directions" button that opens Google Maps. See the Directions page for the full list and map.
City Walk
Our City Walk links nine of the best sites into an easy half-day route, from Fort Cornwallis to the Clan Jetties. Open the Walking Route →
What is UNESCO?
UNESCO named George Town a World Heritage Site in 2008 for its unique multicultural heritage. Learn why we have UNESCO →
Best sites to see
Top picks: the Blue Mansion, Khoo Kongsi, Chew Jetty, Kapitan Keling Mosque and the street art.
Food
Penang is famous for char kway teow, assam laksa and Nyonya food! Little India and the Clan Jetties are great places to eat.
