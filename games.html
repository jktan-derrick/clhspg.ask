The games that children across Malaysia — including George Town — have played for generations. Tap a card for a cultural note.

Team Sport

Sepak Takraw

A fast kick-volleyball played with a woven rattan ball, using feet, knees, chest and head — but never the hands.

Agility

Batu Seremban

A hand-eye coordination game played by tossing and catching five small cloth bags filled with seeds or sand.

Balance

Sepak Bulu Ayam

Players keep a weighted feather shuttlecock in the air using only their feet, counting how many kicks they can manage.

Hopscotch

Teng-Teng

Malaysian hopscotch: players draw numbered grids on the ground and hop through them without touching the lines.

Team Tag

Galah Panjang

An outdoor tag game on a grid court, where attackers try to run past defenders guarding the lines to reach the other end.

Team Racing

Tarik Upih

A fun race where one child sits on a dried betel-nut palm frond while a teammate pulls them across the finish line.

Popular questions
Which is the oldest?
The oldest Chinese temple is the Goddess of Mercy Temple (1728). The oldest Hindu temple is Sri Mahamariamman (1833), and the oldest mosque is the Kapitan Keling Mosque (1801).

What is UNESCO?

Food
Penang is famous for char kway teow, assam laksa and Nyonya food! Little India and the Clan Jetties are great places to eat.
