Popular questions
Which is the oldest?
The oldest Chinese temple is the Goddess of Mercy Temple (1728). The oldest Hindu temple is Sri Mahamariamman (1833), and the oldest mosque is the Kapitan Keling Mosque (1801).
Directions
Every site has a "Get directions" button that opens Google Maps. See the Directions page.
City Walk
Our City Walk links nine of the best sites into an easy half-day route. Open the Walking Route →
What is UNESCO?
UNESCO named George Town a World Heritage Site in 2008 for its multicultural heritage. Learn why we have UNESCO →
Food
Penang is famous for char kway teow, assam laksa and Nyonya food! Little India and the Clan Jetties are great places to eat.
